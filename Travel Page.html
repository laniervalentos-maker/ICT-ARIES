Paysawan

Paysawan is a peaceful coastal paradise in Bagac, Bataan, known for its calm waters, scenic views, and relaxed beach atmosphere.

About Paysawan

A quiet coastal beach known for its natural beauty, simple charm, and peaceful atmosphere that makes every visit feel refreshing.

Why it feels special

Paysawan is a calm and welcoming beach destination in Bagac, Bataan. It is loved for its wide shoreline, clear water, and mountain backdrop that gives the place a peaceful and scenic look.

It is a great spot for travelers who want to enjoy nature without the crowd and noise of bigger resorts.

Golden Shore

Soft sand and bright water that make the beach look warm and inviting.

Quiet Escape

Perfect for relaxing, enjoying the breeze, and escaping the busy city.

Scenic Views

Beautiful coastlines and mountain scenery create a memorable backdrop.

Nature Retreat

Ideal for swimming, walking, camping, and enjoying a slow peaceful day.

Why Visit Paysawan

A laid-back destination that offers comfort, freedom, and beauty without too much fuss.

  • Budget-friendly access: There is no entrance fee, so visitors only pay for the cottage or camping area they use.
  • No corkage fees: Guests can bring their own food and supplies without extra charges.
  • Fewer crowds: The beach feels quieter and more peaceful than busy commercial resorts.
  • Pet-friendly atmosphere: Travelers can enjoy the beach together with their pets.

Location

How to get there

  1. Start from the main highway and head toward Bagac - Mariveles Road.
  2. Pass by Pantingan Elementary School to confirm you are on the right route.
  3. Continue until you reach Barangay Saysain and the local barangay hall.
  4. Look for the welcome arch of Barangay Paysawan and follow the signs leading to the beach.

Proof of Its Beauty
